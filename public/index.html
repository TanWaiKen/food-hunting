How it works

Four steps, two times we ask you first

  1. 01

    Tell us the trip

    City, days, where you sleep, the dishes you must not miss.

  2. 02

    We verify

    Rednote, Google and the web — two sources per place, or it's marked unverified.

  3. 03

    We time it

    Breakfast before the sell-out, supper when the market is open, the walk in between.

  4. 04

    You eat, then keep it

    Upload your photos; they find their place, and each becomes a small abstract memory.
